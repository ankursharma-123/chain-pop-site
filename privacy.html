Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Who operates Chain Pop

Chain Pop is operated by Ankur Sharma. For privacy questions, contact asharma7f@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

  • Game progress and scores are saved in this browser for offline play. When Facebook Instant Games services are available, the game can also save progress using the platform’s player data feature.
  • The game may send gameplay and ad lifecycle events to Facebook Instant Games analytics, such as round results, achievements, leaderboard submissions, and ad events.
  • When enabled, Meta Audience Network placements may request banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads. Meta processes information under its own policies and terms.
  • The currently configured game build does not connect to the optional Chain Pop Go/MongoDB game server. If that service is enabled later, this policy must be updated to describe the data it receives, stores, and how deletion requests are handled.

Storage and deletion

Browser-saved progress remains until browser site data is cleared. Facebook platform data is handled through Facebook Instant Games and Facebook account controls. Contact the operator using the email above for questions or deletion assistance.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the game’s data practices change. For questions, contact asharma7f@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy covers the Chain Pop Android game and the separate Facebook Instant Games version. The services and data practices differ by version, as described below.

Who operates Chain Pop

Chain Pop is operated by Ankur Sharma. For privacy questions, contact asharma7f@gmail.com.

Android version

Game progress

The Android game stores campaign progress, stars, scores, and achievements locally on your device using the app's storage. The current Android build does not require an account and does not send this saved game progress to a Chain Pop server. Local data remains on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

Advertising

The Android game uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. When an ad is requested, shown, or interacted with, Unity and its advertising partners may process technical and advertising information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad delivery or interaction events. They use this information to provide, measure, protect, and, where permitted, personalize advertising. The rewarded ad is optional; it offers extra game time when watched.

Unity's handling of information is described in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Cookie and Advertising Policy. Android advertising ID and ad privacy choices may also be available in your device settings. If additional ad networks are enabled in the game, this policy will be updated to identify them.

Facebook Instant Games version

  • Game progress and scores are saved in browser storage for offline play. When Facebook Instant Games services are available, the game can also save progress using the platform's player data feature.
  • When available, the game may send gameplay and ad lifecycle events to Facebook Instant Games analytics, such as round results, achievements, leaderboard submissions, and ad events.
  • When enabled, Meta Audience Network may process information to serve banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads under Meta's policies and terms.

The Facebook version's platform and advertising data are handled according to the applicable Facebook and Meta privacy settings and policies.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Chain Pop does not currently connect either version to its optional Go/MongoDB game server. Android game progress stays in local app storage; Facebook/browser progress stays in browser storage or Facebook Instant Games player data as described above. To remove Android local progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Browser data can be removed using browser site-data controls. Facebook platform data is subject to Facebook's account and platform controls.

Unity and Meta may retain information they process for advertising or platform services according to their own policies. Contact the operator at asharma7f@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests relating to information handled by Chain Pop.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices or integrated services change. The latest version will be available at this page.
